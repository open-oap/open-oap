<f:layout name="Email/DefaultTXT" />

    Email / Mail for proposal revision status

    Available variables:
        {status} proposal status string
        {proposal} proposal with submodells applicant, call...
        {signature} dynamic builded signature (based upon call data and signature-number of proposal
        {mailsubject} subject of the state record - empty for states without an own subject

This template decides about the subject: FluidEmail renders html first and txt afterwards,
so the subject section of the txt template is the one that wins. Do not add any whitespace
inside it - FluidEmail overrides the subject set in PHP with the rendered section as soon
as it is not empty, and whitespace counts as not empty.

<f:section name="Subject"><f:if condition="{mailsubject}"><f:then>{mailsubject}</f:then><f:else><f:translate key="LLL:EXT:open_oap/Resources/Private/Language/locallang.xlf:tx_openoap.email.status_{proposal.state}.subject" languageKey="{languageKey}" arguments="{0: siteName, 1: signature}"/></f:else></f:if></f:section>

<f:section name="Mailcontent">
    <f:format.stripTags allowedTags="">{mailtext}</f:format.stripTags>
</f:section>
</html>
